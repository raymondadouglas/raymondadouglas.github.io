Chrome: paste this into a new tab (Chrome won’t let a web page link there directly):

chrome://settings/searchEngines

Under Site search, click Add. Call it Meanwhile, give it the shortcut tz, and this URL:

Then type tz, a space, and your question: tz 4pm SF in CZ.

Firefox: right-click the address bar and choose Add “Meanwhile”, then give it a keyword under Settings → Search.

Safari doesn’t allow custom search engines. A bookmark to this page is the next best thing.
