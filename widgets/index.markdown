<h1>Widgets</h1>

Small, single-purpose tools I made because I wanted them to exist and couldn't find versions without ads, sign-ups, or opinions about my cookies. They should all work offline-ish, on a phone, and without asking you for anything.

### The collection

[**Meanwhile**](/widgets/meanwhile/) — a time zone converter you can just ask: *4pm SF in CZ*, *UK time*, *GB to CZ*.

[**Tuner**](/tuner/) — a chromatic tuner with reference tones for guitar, bass, ukulele, violin and cello. Installable as an app if you ask your phone nicely.
